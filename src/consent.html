ChatPro activity tracking

While you are clocked in (and not on a break) in the Beyond Caption Team app, ChatPro records:

  • how many keys you press and how much you use the mouse, per minute (never which keys or what you type)
  • minutes without any input (idle time)
  • which app or website is in front (for example "OnlyMonster" or "YouTube"), never window titles or chat content

Not recorded: anything outside your shift or during breaks, screenshots, typed text, clipboard.
Who sees it: the owner and team leaders, in the ChatPro dashboard. Minute data is deleted after 90 days; daily totals are kept.
You can revoke this at any time in the tray menu or settings. Tracking stops immediately.
